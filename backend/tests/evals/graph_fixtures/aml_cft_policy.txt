ACME MICROFINANCE BANK LIMITED
ANTI-MONEY LAUNDERING AND COMBATING THE FINANCING OF TERRORISM POLICY
Version 3.0 - approved by the Board of Directors on 12 February 2026

1. PURPOSE AND REVIEW

1.1 This policy sets out how the Bank prevents its products and services from being used for money laundering and terrorist financing.
1.2 The Board Audit Committee shall review this policy every year to reflect changes in CBN regulations.

5. CUSTOMER IDENTIFICATION

5.1 The Head of Customer Service shall ensure that no account is activated until the customer's Bank Verification Number (BVN) has been obtained and validated on the NIBSS platform.
5.2 The Head of Operations shall place every account without a linked BVN on 'post no debit' status and shall lift the restriction only after a valid BVN has been obtained.

6. REGULATORY REPORTING

6.1 The Chief Compliance Officer shall render the monthly AML/CFT returns, including the politically exposed persons and risk assessment reports, to the CBN on or before the 14th day after the end of each month, and shall render a NIL return where there is nothing to report.
6.2 The Chief Compliance Officer shall file Suspicious Transaction Reports with the NFIU within 24 hours of a suspicion being formed.

7. TRAINING

7.1 The Head of Human Resources shall organise annual AML/CFT training for all staff and keep the signed attendance register.

8. MARKETING

8.3 The Marketing Unit shall feature the Bank's BVN-linked mobile application in its quarterly newspaper advertisements.

DATA PROTECTION AND PRIVACY POLICY
Version 1.2

3. DATA SUBJECT RIGHTS

3.1 The Data Protection Officer shall respond to every data subject access request within 30 days of receiving it.
3.2 The Data Protection Officer shall keep a register of all data subject requests and the responses given.

4. RETENTION

4.1 The Records Manager shall delete marketing contact lists that have not been used for two years.

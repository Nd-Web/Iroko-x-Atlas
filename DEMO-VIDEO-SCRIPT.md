# Iroko AI × MTN — Demo Video Script

**Target length: 7–8 minutes.** Short enough that an executive actually finishes it; long enough to land every feature.

**The video's one job:** by the end, the viewer thinks *"our people waste hours every week on things this does in seconds — and it catches compliance risks we currently find out about from the regulator."*

---

## Production notes (read before recording)

- **Record with the backend warm.** Open irokoai.site 10 minutes before, log in, run one chat question to wake Render. Never record a cold start.
- **Cut the thinking time.** Live answers take 20–45 seconds — in the video, cut to the moment the answer streams in. This is your biggest advantage over a live demo: Iroko looks instant.
- **Record in one browser window, full screen, 1080p or better.** Hide bookmarks bar. Use the demo login so the data is fully populated.
- **Voiceover:** record narration separately over the screen capture if you can — it always sounds cleaner than talking while clicking.
- **The meeting-bot scene needs a second person** (or you on a phone as a second participant) in a real Teams call, speaking a question aloud. Record the meeting audio — hearing Iroko's voice answer is the wow moment. Do a test run first; keep the take where the answer lands cleanly.
- **Numbers in this script are real** — they come from the seeded dataset and verified regulations. Don't improvise new ones.

## SCENE 1 — The problem (0:00–0:45)

**On screen:** Not the product yet. A slide or plain dark screen with the words as you say them. Then cut to a fast montage: folders of PDFs, an email inbox, a SharePoint list, a WhatsApp thread.

**Voiceover:**

> "MTN Nigeria runs on documents. Vendor contracts. Network incident reports. NCC quality-of-service returns. NDPA data-protection records. Customer complaint logs. Thousands of them — spread across mailboxes, shared drives, and departmental silos.
>
> When an engineer needs to know what a vendor SLA actually promises, or legal needs to know whether a product launch breaches an NCC directive — someone goes searching. It takes hours. Sometimes days. Sometimes the answer arrives after the deadline.
>
> This is Iroko AI: an intelligence layer that reads every document your organisation has — and turns it into answers, actions, and accountability. In the next seven minutes, you'll see exactly what it does."

---

## SCENE 2 — Ask anything, get a cited answer (0:45–2:00)

**On screen:** Log in → land on Dashboard for 2 seconds (let the viewer register the live stats) → go to **Chat**.

**Type:** `What is the minimum network availability required by NCC QoS benchmarks, and are any of our clusters currently below it?`

**Cut the wait. Show the answer streaming in, then hold on the citations.**

**Voiceover:**

> "This is the core of Iroko. Ask a question in plain English — and a team of AI agents goes to work. One retrieves the relevant documents. One reasons over them. One checks the answer against the evidence before you ever see it.
>
> The NCC requires 99% network availability and a 95% call-setup success rate. And Iroko doesn't stop at the regulation — it cross-references our own network reports and flags that the Ikeja cluster is running at 82.7%. Below benchmark.
>
> Notice what's under the answer: citations. Every claim links to the exact document it came from. Iroko is not allowed to make things up — if the evidence isn't in your documents, it says so. That's the difference between a chatbot and an intelligence system."

**Optional 10-second beat:** click one citation to open the source document.

---

## SCENE 3 — The compliance brain (2:00–3:15)

**On screen:** Go to **Compliance** → the Live Compliance Check box.

**Type:** `Activate 5,000 SIM cards without NIN verification to hit our quarterly sales target`

**Cut to the verdict appearing: NO-GO.**

**Voiceover:**

> "Now the feature built for a regulated operator. This is the Live Compliance Check — describe any action, product, or policy, and Iroko checks it against NCC, NDPA, and FCCPC regulations in real time.
>
> Activating SIMs without NIN verification? Iroko says NO-GO — and explains why: it breaches the NCC SIM Registration Regulations, carries a penalty of two hundred thousand naira *per SIM* — that's a one-billion-naira exposure on five thousand SIMs — and it cites the precedent: the 2015 SIM registration fine of one point zero four *trillion* naira. The largest regulatory fine in African telecom history. Against MTN.
>
> Every employee can check before they act. The compliance team stops being a bottleneck — and starts being a system."

**On screen while saying the last line:** scroll to show the verdict's cited regulations.

## SCENE 4 — Insights become actions automatically (3:15–4:15)

**On screen:** Go to **Workflows**. The board is already populated. Point out a task tagged from the compliance verdict, with department + SLA countdown.

**Voiceover:**

> "Here's where Iroko goes beyond every document tool you've seen: it closes the loop.
>
> That NO-GO verdict didn't just appear on a screen — it automatically became a task. Routed to the right department. With an SLA deadline attached, based on severity. The same happens when Iroko detects a network anomaly, an expiring contract, or a regulatory filing coming due.
>
> This board is the difference between *knowing* and *doing*. Nothing Iroko finds can be quietly ignored — every insight has an owner, a deadline, and a status. Your problem statement asked for workflow management driven by intelligence. This is it, live."

**On screen:** click **Run Intelligence Sweep** → new tasks appear → hold 3 seconds.

---

## SCENE 5 — 🎤 The showstopper: Iroko joins your meeting (4:15–5:45)

**On screen:** Split or sequential: (1) the Compliance page's "Send Iroko to a meeting" panel — paste a real Teams link → **Join meeting**; (2) cut to the actual Teams call, where "Iroko AI" appears as a participant and greets the room aloud.

**Then — the moment.** A colleague in the call asks, out loud:

> *"Iroko — can we retain subscriber call detail records for five years without explicit consent?"*

**Let the room go quiet. Iroko answers ALOUD in the call, in a Nigerian voice, citing the NDPA.** Keep the take where this lands cleanly.

**Voiceover (after the moment):**

> "That just happened. Iroko joined the meeting from a pasted link, listened to the conversation, heard a compliance question — and answered out loud, with the regulation, in the room.
>
> No dashboard. No typing. Your compliance intelligence now sits in the meeting where the decisions are actually being made.
>
> No other tool on the market does this today — not Copilot, not any enterprise search product. This is Iroko in the room with your team."

And it has never joined a Teams meeting and answered a question out loud.

**The one-liner, if you only get one sentence:**

> *"Azure AI Search is our retrieval engine — Iroko is everything that happens after retrieval: verdicts, actions, accountability, and a voice in your meetings."*
